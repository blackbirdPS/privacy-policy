Privacy Policy

Kadi Workout App  ·  Last updated: April 25, 2026

Kadi does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We never see it.

Who We Are

Kadi is a workout tracking application developed and published as an independent app. References to "we," "us," or "our" in this policy refer to the developer of Kadi.

Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. Kadi is designed as a fully offline app. All data you enter — including your display name, workout sessions, exercise logs, personal records, body weight entries, and goals — is stored exclusively on your device using your device's local storage (SQLite database).

This means:

  • No account creation is required.
  • No data is transmitted to any server.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • No advertising networks are used.
  • We have no access to any information you enter into the app.

Data Stored on Your Device

Kadi stores the following data locally on your device solely for the purpose of providing app functionality:

  • A display name you provide during setup
  • Your fitness goal and experience level preferences
  • Your preferred weight units (lbs or kg)
  • Workout sessions, sets, reps, and weight entries
  • Personal records and strength goals
  • Bodyweight log entries
  • App settings such as theme preference and rest timer durations

This data never leaves your device and is only accessible to the Kadi app installed on your device.

Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all Kadi data at any time by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling Kadi permanently removes all locally stored data.

Third-Party Services

Kadi does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or data brokers. The app operates entirely offline and independently of any external service.

Children's Privacy

Kadi does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no personal data from any user, it poses no special risk to younger users. However, the app is intended for general fitness use and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future, particularly if a future version of Kadi introduces cloud features or data syncing. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

If future versions of the app collect personal data, we will seek your explicit consent before doing so and will update this policy accordingly.

Your Rights

Since Kadi does not collect or store any personal data on external servers, there is no data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. All data management is handled directly on your device.

If you are a California resident, please note that Kadi does not sell personal information and does not share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
